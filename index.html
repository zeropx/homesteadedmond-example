Welcome

Welcome to Homestead

Located in northwest Edmond, Oklahoma, the Homestead community—proudly managed by the Homestead Property Owners’ Association, Inc.—is home to 273 families.

Our neighborhood offers a peaceful and family-friendly environment with two fishing ponds, a community pool, and two playgrounds. With easy access to shopping, dining, and several nearby universities, Homestead is a wonderful place to call home—whether you’re just starting out or looking for a comfortable place to retire.
